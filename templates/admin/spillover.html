Spillover Student Management

Students who have exceeded the standard 4-year graduation period

What is Spillover? A student becomes a spillover when they fail to complete their programme within the approved duration (4 years for B.Sc.). Reasons include: carry-over courses, missing exams, academic suspension, or insufficient credit units. Spillover students must register for and complete outstanding courses before graduation clearance.
